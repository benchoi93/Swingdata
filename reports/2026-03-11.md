### 5. Schoenfeld Residual Direction (Reviewer Recommendation)
- **Script**: Same `bootstrap_mediation.py`
- **Output**: Same JSON file
- Results for tub_frac covariate:

| Endpoint | Spearman rho | p-value | Direction |
|----------|-------------|---------|-----------|
| first_harsh | -0.115 | 6e-118 | HR decreasing |
| first_high_cv | -0.051 | 8e-25 | HR decreasing |
| first_speeding | -0.148 | 3e-146 | HR decreasing |

- **Key finding**: TUB hazard ratios *attenuate* over the trip sequence, not intensify.
- **Correction**: Previous discussion.tex stated "the TUB effect likely intensifies as users gain experience" — this was speculation, now corrected with data. The attenuation is substantively plausible: early TUB adoption represents the highest-risk transition; experienced TUB users develop familiarity with the vehicle.
- ever_tub results are mixed (first_high_cv is increasing), but tub_frac direction is consistently negative.
