### PH Assumption Check (New Analysis)
- Created `src/v2/ph_check.py`: Schoenfeld residual tests for all 3 Cox PH survival models
- Results: 5/6 covariate-endpoint combinations violate PH (p < 0.001)
  - `first_harsh`: ever_tub χ²=240.0, tub_frac χ²=84.3 (both violated)
  - `first_high_cv`: ever_tub χ²=73.4, tub_frac χ²=131.7 (both violated)
  - `first_speeding`: ever_tub χ²=1.42 p=0.234 (OK), tub_frac χ²=558.1 (violated)
- Interpretation: Large N (127K) makes Schoenfeld tests highly powered; violations are substantively plausible (TUB effect intensifies with experience). HRs interpreted as average effects, consistent with KM curves.
