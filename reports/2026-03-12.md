## Key Findings

| Outcome | GEE coef (STD) | FE coef (STD) | Change |
|---------|---------------|--------------|--------|
| Harsh accel | -0.610 | -0.520 | -14.8% (attenuated) |
| Harsh decel | -0.627 | -0.490 | -21.8% (attenuated) |
| Speed CV | -0.022 | -0.089 | +301% (amplified) |
| Cruise frac | +0.061 | +0.112 | +82% (amplified) |
| Zero-speed | -0.008 | -0.041 | +416% (amplified) |
| Mean speed | -1.942 | -0.855 | -56% (attenuated) |

**Interpretation**: Safety mode effects survive FE but attenuate ~15-22%, confirming user self-selection inflates GEE. Dynamics effects amplify dramatically under FE, revealing that GEE underestimates within-user governance effects on riding patterns due to between-user heterogeneity.
