### Phase 7C.1: Land Use Data Research
Evaluated 11+ data sources across 4 categories for adding land use controls at trip-origin level across 52 South Korean cities. Full report: `reports/land_use_data_research.md`.

**Korean Government Sources Evaluated:**
1. **MOLIT 토지이용현황도** (land cover map) — parcel-level, nationwide, but only 6 land COVER categories (urban/forest/agriculture). Cannot distinguish residential/commercial/industrial. NOT recommended.
2. **MOLIT 토지이용계획정보** (zoning plan data) — parcel-level zoning with residential/commercial/industrial/green subcategories. BEST match for our needs. Requires cadastral boundary polygons for spatial join.
3. **토지이음** (Land E-um portal) — per-parcel API lookup. Infeasible at 19.5M scale.
4. **SGIS** (Statistics Korea) — business census + population at 100m grid. Excellent land use proxy via establishment density by sector.
5. **VWorld WFS API** — has zoning data but rate-limited and export-restricted.
6. **Building Register API** — per-building use type, but API-only (scale infeasible).
7. **LH** — covers LH developments only, not nationwide.

**OSM Coverage (Empirical Testing):**

| City | Landuse polygons | Area coverage | Trip match rate |
|------|-----------------|--------------|----------------|
| Seoul | 4,778 | 23.0% | 14.5% |
| Daejeon | 4,636 | 9.0% | Not tested |

- Building footprints: 99.6% of trips have ≥1 building within 200m, BUT 68.3% tagged "yes" (unclassified type)
- POI/amenity: excellent coverage (4,914 in small Seoul bbox), best OSM-based proxy
- **Conclusion**: OSM landuse polygons insufficient for Korean cities

**Alternative Sources:**
- WorldPop 100m population grid — easy, free, but density-only (no use type)
- GHS-SMOD 1km settlement classification — too coarse
- Kakao POI via H3 aggregation — feasible (8,144 hex queries), well-established in transport lit
- VIIRS night lights — too coarse (500m)

**Recommended Approaches (ranked):**
1. **MOLIT 토지이용계획정보** — gold standard zoning, 2-3 day effort
2. **SGIS Business + Population Census** — practical proxy, 2-3 day effort
3. **WorldPop + OSM Landuse** — quick (1 day) but limited
4. **Kakao POI via H3 + WorldPop** — practical hybrid, 1-2 days

**Minimum viable**: WorldPop 100m population density only (30 min to integrate, stated as limitation).

## Key Findings

1. **OSM is NOT viable for Korean land use classification** — only 14.5% trip match in Seoul (best case), building types 68.3% unclassified
2. **MOLIT zoning (토지이용계획정보)** is the best source — exact residential/commercial/industrial classification at parcel level
3. **SGIS business census** is the most practical alternative — 100m grid business establishment density by sector
4. **WorldPop** is the minimum viable option — just population density, can be done in 30 minutes
5. **POI density** (Kakao or OSM) is feasible via H3 aggregation — well-established in e-scooter literature
